05Your data and your backups

Everything Ursa keeps is on your Mac, encrypted, and we have no copy (see the privacy policy, /ursa/privacy/). That means:

  • We can't recover your data if your Mac is lost, damaged or erased, or if you delete it.

  • An export (Settings › Data › Export) is not encrypted. Anyone who can open it can read it.
